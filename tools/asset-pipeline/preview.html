Apparel Preview

Composites your sprite on the real openpiece body (same ANATOMY constants as drawActor). Dial in the fit, copy the def values. No game launch.

The body frame maps the WHOLE square canvas to ±1.5r. If your sprite was trimmed tight by the pipeline, switch to a NON-trimmed export or use item-frame — trimming breaks body-frame positioning. Head/torso/shoulders drawn exactly as the game does.
