Every battle, chest, and quest in Krucia provides rewards that drive progression. Loot strengthens
your captain and crew, unlocks upgrades, and expands your options at sea.

## Sources of loot

Loot can be obtained from many places. Defeating enemy ships, opening treasure chests on islands or
beneath the waves, completing quests, exploring shipwrecks and caves, and even random sea events such
as drifting barrels or washed-up crates can all yield valuable rewards.

## Core loot types

- **Gold** — used for hiring crew, purchasing upgrades, unlocking quests, cosmetics, enchantments, and
  pursuing the main objective.
- **Schematics** — plans for ship upgrades, unlocking cannons, sails, hull reinforcements, and other
  permanent improvements.
- **Enchantments** — permanent stat boosts for crew, improving combat, sailing, and abilities.
- **Cosmetics** — visual customization for ship, captain, and crew. Purely aesthetic.
- **Resources** — trade goods with varying values between islands. Profitable when sold at the right
  port.
- **Consumables / Temporary Buffs** — short-term benefits for ship or crew, useful in battles, storms,
  or long voyages.

## Experience rewards

In addition to physical items, you also gain experience. You and your crew members gain XP from
battles and quests, allowing them to level up, improve their stats, and strengthen their gifts over
time.

## Loot rarity and distribution

The rarity and distribution of loot vary across the seas. Common rewards like gold and resources are
frequent, while schematics, enchantments, and cosmetics are rarer and more exciting. Some items are
tied to specific enemies, regions, or quests, making them more difficult to obtain.
