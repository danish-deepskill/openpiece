# About Sealubbers

> A pirate RPG built as an independent long-term project.
> Source: [sealubbers.com](https://sealubbers.com) About page, captured July 2026.

Sealubbers is an open-world pirate RPG set in **Krucia**, an ocean world built around treasure
hunting, crew building, ship combat, exploration, and the idea of leaving your own story written into
the stars.

The game and site are being developed independently under **Plunder LLC**. The site exists to explain
the world, show active progress, and give people one place to follow along as the project grows.

## What the project is trying to do

The goal is to make a pirate adventure that feels expansive without becoming impersonal. That means
the world needs memorable places, factions with their own identity, crew members who matter, and
systems that connect back to the fantasy of becoming a captain rather than just clearing objectives.

Sealubbers is meant to reward curiosity. Sailing to a strange island, recruiting someone unexpected,
tracking down treasure clues, or choosing how to handle a dangerous ship should all feel like part of
the same larger journey.

- **The game** — an open-world pirate RPG focused on exploration, crew progression, quests, treasure
  hunting, and naval combat.
- **The site** — a home for development updates, world details, system breakdowns, and the
  public-facing information around the project.
- **The approach** — independent development with an emphasis on making the game feel distinctive
  rather than rushing it out before the core ideas feel right.

## Who is behind it

Sealubbers is being developed independently by **Captain Rubin** through Plunder LLC. This is not a
large studio project with a separate marketing site and publishing arm behind it. The development
work, updates, and public communication all come from the same small operation.

That is also why the site is intentionally direct. It is there to show what the game is, what progress
is being made, and where people can keep up with it, without turning the project into something that
feels overproduced or impersonal.

## How to follow along

The best place to see real progress is the development section, where larger updates are posted when
there is something meaningful to show. The support page links out to the main social channels for
shorter updates, clips, and community activity between bigger posts.
