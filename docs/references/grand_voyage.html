Grand Voyage

A fan-made pirate prototype · sail, fight, and build a crew

Movement & Aim

WASDMove MouseAim (your character faces the cursor) ShiftDodge roll — brief invulnerability, costs stamina EInteract: talk, hire, eat the fruit, board / leave boat

Combat

L-Click/JAttack — short punch, or Gomu Pistol after the fruit KGomu Gatling — rapid cone barrage (fruit only) R-Click/LGomu Rocket — launch dash through enemies (fruit only)

Crew Command

QOrder crew to attack (toward your cursor / nearest foe) FToggle crew stance: Follow ↔ Hold position HShow / hide this help

Tip: find the tavern to recruit pirates, eat the fruit by the big tree, then take the boat east.
