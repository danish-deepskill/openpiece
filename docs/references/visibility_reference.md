# Marine Notoriety System — Notes

*You are a pirate. **Notoriety** is how aware the Marines and the World Government are of you and your crew.*

## What Is Notoriety?

Notoriety measures **how much heat the Marines have on you right now** — how actively the World Government is hunting your crew. It reflects the growing attention the Marines pay to you as you become a bigger threat.

- When you first set sail, you're a nobody. The Marines have no record of you.
- As you raid, plunder, and gain infamy, your Notoriety climbs and the Marines close in.

**Core rules**
- Notoriety is a number between **0 and 100**.
- It **cools off by 1 every day** when you keep a low profile.
- Certain jobs and exploits **raise** it; others (lying low, bribing officials, false trails) **lower** it.

> **Modifier meanings**
> - **Black Market cost** — how much everything costs through the Underworld (contraband + jobs). Higher = worse.
> - **Contraband wait** — how long you wait for a smuggler's dead-drop job to appear. Higher = worse.
> - **Contraband window** — how long the dead-drop stays available for pickup. Higher = better.

## Stage Details

### 👁️ Unknown — `0–20`
You're just another face in a crowded port. The Marines have no record of you and no reason to care. They **won't respond** to raids on their bases or outposts, and no patrol will come looking for you across the seas.

- **Black Market cost:** 1x
- **Contraband wait:** 0.5x
- **Contraband window:** 5x
- **Marine response:** N/A — None
- **Special effects:** None

---

### 👁️ Whispers — `21–40`
Word of your exploits drifts through dockside taverns and gossip, but to the Marines you're still a rookie nuisance. Local bases scramble a **patrol** when one of their outposts is hit, though you'll usually be long gone before they arrive. Dock inspections tighten, making contraband **slightly harder** to move.

- **Black Market cost:** 1x
- **Contraband wait:** 1x
- **Contraband window:** 2x
- **Marine response:** 5:00 — Marine Patrol *(5–10 soldiers arriving by ship)*
- **Special effects:** None

---

### 👁️ Rumors — `41–60`
Rumors of your crew reach Marine **officers**. No hard proof yet, but they're taking it seriously and beefing up security. Expect stronger garrisons at Marine installations and an **officer strike squad** the moment an alarm sounds. Marine patrols now stop and search every ship they pass.

- **Black Market cost:** 1x
- **Contraband wait:** 2x
- **Contraband window:** 1x
- **Marine response:** 4:00 — Officer Strike Squad *(5–10 Marine officers arriving by ship)*
- **Special effects:**
  - Marine convoy event can now appear *(a supply ship you can raid)*
  - All Marine forces are **20% larger**

---

### 👁️ Headlines — `61–80`
The **News Coo** dropped your wanted poster on doorsteps across the seas — you're front-page news. The World Government now brands you a **serious threat** and makes hunting you a priority. Security is up everywhere, Marines raid any island suspected of harboring you, and a propaganda campaign chokes off your Underworld supply lines and local goodwill.

- **Black Market cost:** 2x
- **Contraband wait:** 5x
- **Contraband window:** 0.5x
- **Marine response:** 3:00 — Vice Admiral's Guard *(8–16, mix of ranged and melee)*
- **Special effects:**
  - Marine convoy event can now appear
  - All Marine forces are **50% larger**
  - Marine raids happen **twice as often**
  - Runaway Recruit quest **no longer occurs**
  - World Government propaganda drop event can now appear

---

### 👁️ Most Wanted — `81–99`
Your name is spoken in every port; the World Government calls you their **number one enemy**. They'll spend anything to sink you — including scorched-earth shelling of whole islands suspected of sheltering you, and they'll level their own outposts to catch you. **Cannon fire** answers every act of aggression. Smuggling all but dries up. **Cipher Pol** spreads disinformation to turn other crews against you, until you can't tell ally from ambush. This is all-out war.

- **Black Market cost:** 5x
- **Contraband wait:** 10x
- **Contraband window:** 0.5x
- **Marine response:** 2:00 — Warship Barrage *(cannon fire targets your crew on the quest location every 5 seconds)*
- **Special effects:**
  - Marine convoy event can now appear
  - All Marine forces are **100% larger**
  - Marine raids happen **three times as often**
  - Runaway Recruit quest **no longer occurs**
  - World Government propaganda drop event can now appear
  - **Pacifista** bombardment event can now appear
  - **Cipher Pol (CP0)** raid event can now appear
  - Relations with random crews/factions drop by **-10 once a day**

---

### ☠️ Buster Call — `100`
The Fleet Admiral has authorized a **Buster Call** on your position. You have **30 days** before a full task force — an Admiral, ten warships, and a line of Vice Admirals — converges on your island.

> ⚠️ **Unless you bring your Notoriety back down to Unknown in time, the island and everyone on it will be wiped from the map.**

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Low profile is survival.** Notoriety cools passively (−1/day), so ducking into a quiet stretch of sea naturally lets the heat die down.
- **The Underworld squeezes you from both ends** as you get famous: smuggler jobs take forever to show up (0.5x → 10x wait), and the pickup window shrinks (5x → 0.5x). Stock up on contraband *early*, while you're still a nobody.
- **Marine response times shorten each stage** (5:00 → 2:00), and forces grow in size *and* firepower — from a handful of grunts by ship to constant cannon barrages, Pacifistas, and CP0.
- **61+ (Headlines) is the turning point:** once the News Coo publishes you, Underworld costs double, Marine raids double, and recruitment opportunities dry up. Crossing into the papers is a one-way jump in difficulty.
- **100 is a fail-state on a clock, not a plateau.** Buster Call is a 30-day countdown to total annihilation — the only play is to *drop your heat fast*, not to fight it head-on.
