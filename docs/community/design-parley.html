Parley

status: charted — talking as real play, planned

Parley: the old pirate right to talk before anyone draws. In Openpiece, talking is playing — words are verbs with consequences, not a wall of text between you and the fight.

Words as verbs

Persuade the guard he didn't see you. Threaten a captain into striking his colors without a shot — if your name carries the weight to back it (see Bounty). Recruit the swordsman sulking in the tavern. Bribe, bargain, bluff, betray. Every one of them a real move with a real cost, read against who you're talking to and what they know of you.

Information moves by mouth

Rumors, directions, warnings, lies — most of what you'll ever know about the sea arrives in conversation. The tavern is an instrument panel, if you know how to read one (see Exploration for what that knowledge is worth).

Where it stands

The world already speaks in-engine — basic dialogue and interaction verbs run today. The full social game — persuasion, favors, standing, betrayal — is charted, and it grows alongside the crew and bounty systems it leans on.

Some doors only words open. Some fights are won before they start.
