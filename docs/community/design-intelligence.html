Intelligence

status: designed, the intelligence ladder is on paper; basic minds run today

Every enemy runs on the same brain. What differs is how much of it they're allowed to use. A dockside thug and a marine captain aren't different code, they're different minds: one rushes you in a straight line, the other keeps his reach, feints, cuts off your escape, and counters the trick that worked on the last three men.

No wallhacks. No aimbots. If a captain finds you, it's because someone saw you, or he guessed well.

The intelligence ladder

Smarts is a dial from 1 to 10, set per character as data. Climb it and watch the same fight change species:

Rank is a warning label you learn to respect.

The same swing, three fighters

What a rung actually buys, watching one attack land on three defenders:

The recruit

Sees the swing late. Gets his guard up clumsily or eats it. Blocks whatever comes, because blocking is all he knows.

The veteran

Reads the windup early. Knows this one wants a parry, not a block. Steps his dodge to the safe side.

The ace

Reads the feint. Your fake windup doesn't move him. And the third time you try your favorite trick, he's already where the real swing lands.

Fair minds only.AI reads the world through the same senses you do: vision cones, hearing, the dark of night. Feint with no rhythm and you beat the ace honestly. That's the whole duel, in both directions.

The gym

Minds are tuned the way fighters are: in a training ring nobody watches.

  • A scenario league pits intelligence tiers against each other, thousands of duels at a time
  • "A 7 beats a 4" becomes a measured fact, not a hope
  • A mind is promoted to the live game only when the ring says it earned it

Where it stands

Simple minds run in-engine today (states, profiles, squad hunts). The full ladder, the counter-play, and the gym are designed and waiting their turn in the queue.

Outsmarting an enemy should feel like outsmarting someone.
