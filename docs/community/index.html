A fan-made pirate world · free, forever

Openpiece

Start a nobody with a rowboat. End a name that moves the sea. Openpiece is a One Piece-inspired open-world pirate sim: a living, deeply-simulated ocean of real sailing, layered Devil Fruit combat, and crews who are real people, running whether you watch it or not. The world doesn't know you're special. That's the point.

Already afloat

Running today, in-engine, not concept art:

Sail a real sea

Read the wind off the water, trim to your point of sail, drop a true anchor, lash the wheel and walk away. Get caught head to wind and work your way out like a sailor.

Walk your own deck

Decks are real places: cross the gangplank, take the wheel, get launched off the stern if you're careless. Three hulls that genuinely handle differently.

Build your fighter

A rubber-fist flurry, a cutlass, and a fighting style on one body: Devil Fruit + weapon + style compose through one engine. The Gomu fruit is in end to end, beside swords, guns, and dirty brawling.

Cross a living island

Factions with real relations, townsfolk who defend themselves, wildlife that flees or gores, squads that hunt together. And you can possess any character you meet.

An engine that can't lie

Fully deterministic simulation: same seed, same world, proven by replay tests. Everything data-driven, ~480 automated tests, built from day one so multiplayer stays possible.

Where the voyage stands

Development is charted as islands on a route, PoC → Prototype → MVP → Production → beyond, by milestones, not dates. The ship is currently anchored off Island 0: The Proof (the PoC), one capstone from weighing anchor for Island 1: Find the Fun (the Prototype, ship combat, crews, the fun test). Nothing is released yet; everything shown runs in-engine.
