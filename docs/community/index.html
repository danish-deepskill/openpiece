Openpiece

A One Piece-inspired open-world pirate sim — a living, deeply-simulated sea you rise through from a nobody with a rowboat to a legend with a fleet. Real sailing, layered combat, crews that are real people, and a world that runs whether you're watching or not.

Already afloat

Running today, in-engine — not concept art:

A real sailing sim

Wind with moods and gusts, points of sail per rig, sail states, a real anchor, a wheel you can lash, leeway — and getting caught in irons.

Walkable ships

Decks are real places — walk the planks, cross the gangplank, take the wheel, get launched off the stern if you're careless. Three hulls with genuinely different handling.

Layered combat

Devil Fruit + weapon + fighting style compose through one engine — the Gomu fruit is in end-to-end, alongside swords, guns, and dirty brawling.

A living island

Factions with real relations, townsfolk who defend themselves, wildlife that flees or gores, squads that hunt together — and you can possess any character.

An honest engine

Fully deterministic simulation (same seed, same world — proven by replay tests), everything data-driven, ~480 automated tests. Built from day one so multiplayer stays possible.

Where the voyage stands

Development is charted as islands on a route — PoC → Prototype → MVP → Production → beyond, by milestones, not dates. The ship is currently anchored off Island 0: The Proof (the PoC), one capstone from weighing anchor for Island 1: Find the Fun (the Prototype — ship combat, crews, the fun test). Nothing is released yet; everything shown runs in-engine.
