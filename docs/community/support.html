How to support the voyage

Openpiece is free forever, nothing here is for sale. What helps most costs nothing:

Playtest when the call goes out

The next milestone is literally named "Find the Fun", honest first impressions from strangers are its fuel. Playtests will be announced when Island 1 docks.

Bring your sea stories

Sailed something real? Played every pirate game alive? Opinions on what makes crews, wind, and boarding actually fun are design input, not noise.

Tell a friend who'd care

The project grows at the speed of the people watching it. One share to the right deckhand beats a hundred impressions.

Hold the standard

When channels open: call out what feels wrong. This project's whole method is honest verdicts over comfortable ones, the community should hold it to that too.

On donations

Openpiece is free, forever, no price tag, no paywalls, no paid anything. That part is carved into the mast and will never change. But if the voyage means something to you and you want to toss a coin toward the developer's lamp oil, donations are welcome. They buy nothing: no perks, no priority, no early access, no say the rest of the crew doesn't have, the game stays identical for everyone. Feedback is still the most valuable thing you can give; a donation is just a way of saying "keep sailing."

And when multiplayer comes, donations get their one honest job: shared seas will run on public meters, each server's real monthly cost and every coin toward it visible to all. The month a meter fills, that sea sails, for everyone, donor or not (see Multiplayer).

Donation details will be posted when the community channels open.

Channels: community spaces open when Island 1 docks, this site is the log until then.
