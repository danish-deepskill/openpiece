How to support the voyage

Not with money — Openpiece is free forever and takes none. What actually helps:

Playtest when the call goes out

The next milestone is literally named "Find the Fun" — honest first impressions from strangers are its fuel. Playtests will be announced when Island 1 docks.

Bring your sea stories

Sailed something real? Played every pirate game alive? Opinions on what makes crews, wind, and boarding actually fun are design input, not noise.

Tell a friend who'd care

The project grows at the speed of the people watching it. One share to the right deckhand beats a hundred impressions.

Hold the standard

When channels open: call out what feels wrong. This project's whole method is honest verdicts over comfortable ones — the community should hold it to that too.

Why no donations?

Openpiece is built on love for someone else's world. Taking money — even donations — for a fan work built on One Piece wouldn't be right, and keeping the project clean of it is what lets it exist at all. If you want to give something, give feedback. It's worth more anyway.

Channels: community spaces open when Island 1 docks — this site is the log until then.
